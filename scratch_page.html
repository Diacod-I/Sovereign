Sovereign gives every AI agent a wallet with rules. With budgets, allowlists, approvals, it transacts on its own without going off the rails.

How it works
01

Set the rules once

Give each agent a budget, an allowlist, and an approval threshold.

02

Agents pay on their own

03

Big spends wait for you

Anything over the line pauses for a one-tap human approval.

04

Everything is on record

Reputation and audit live on-chain, not in a black box.
